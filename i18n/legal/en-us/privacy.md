# Login Example Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 4, 2026**

This policy explains how **Login Example** (the “Project”) handles information. The Project is an open-source OAuth demonstration and is not intended to collect or retain personal information as a production service.

## 1. Information handling

The default implementation uses third-party authorization codes and access tokens only to complete the demonstration flow. It does not intentionally store, sell, share, or analyze user profiles. A person or organization deploying a modified instance may process information differently and is responsible for its own policy.

## 2. Third-party services

GitHub, Microsoft, Google, and Cloudflare process information under their own policies. Review the applicable provider policy before authorizing access. The Project is not affiliated with those providers.

## 3. Cookies and security

Functional cookies may maintain the demo session and short-lived OAuth or Turnstile state. HTTPS and secure secret management are required for deployments. You can remove or block cookies, but login may stop working.

## 4. Children, updates, and contact

The Project is intended for developers and learners. Minors should use it with a guardian’s consent. Policy changes will be published with an updated date. Questions may be submitted through the Project’s GitHub Issues or Pull Requests.
